TSD Rally Instruction Generator

Type or load your route, choose an output style, and generate a printable sheet.

Input format reference

Enter one item per line, with fields separated by |. Trailing fields can be left off.

Line typeFormat
Rally instructionmileage | instruction | speed | pause | suffix | general
Odometer check or transit zone instructionmileage | instruction | | | suffix | general  (no speed or pause; not timed; no checkpoints)
Out point (ends an odometer check or transit zone and starts a timed leg)OUTPOINT | mileage | instruction | out time | speed | suffix | general
Section headers[ODOMETER CHECK]   [TRANSIT]   [RALLY] — use as many sections as you need, in order (lines with no header are rally lines)
Commenta line starting with # is ignored
Field / markerWhat it does
Speed (mph)Applies from that mileage on. Printed in the instruction as CAST 30.
PauseSeconds (30), mm:ss, or minutes (2m); printed as Pause 30. Or NEXTMIN for a Richta next-minute out: the car leaves at the next whole minute after it arrives (printed as Next minute out; the rally master sheet shows the out time).
SuffixExtra text printed at the end of the instruction, after the pause and CAST — e.g. FLR. Shown to participants.
GeneralRally master only — never printed for participants. Put CP in it to make the line a checkpoint: it is highlighted and labeled CP on the master sheet, gets no instruction number, and is left off the participant sheet entirely (so don't put speeds, pauses or suffixes on it).
Out timeA clock time (09:51:00) or +20 for 20 minutes after the section starts. The first section counts from the Start time below; later transit zones count from when the car leaves the preceding timed section. For car-specific times, say so in the instruction (e.g. "9:30 + car number + 20 minutes").
@ before a mileage@4.20 shows that mileage on the rally master output only.
**word**Prints the words between the asterisks in bold. Pause and CAST are bolded automatically.
\n in an instructionStarts a new line. Use \n\n for a blank line between paragraphs.

Files: load a .txt file in this format, or a .csv with the columns mileage, instruction, speed, pause, suffix, general (header row optional; cells may contain line breaks). Use Save as text file to keep your route for later.

Instruction numbers run on continuously through the odometer check, rally sections and transit zones.
