Line types

Enter one item per line, with fields separated by |. Trailing fields can be left off.

Line typeFormat
Rally instructionmileage | instruction | speed | pause | suffix | general
Odometer check or transit zone instructionmileage | instruction | | | suffix | general  (no speed or pause; not timed; no checkpoints)
Out point (ends an odometer check or transit zone and starts a timed leg)OUTPOINT | mileage | instruction | out time | speed | suffix | general
NoteNOTE | text | general — text in the instruction column only: no number, mileage, speed, pause or suffix, and no time calculation. Works in any section; general is an optional rally master note.
Section headers[ODOMETER CHECK]   [TRANSIT]   [RALLY] — use as many sections as you need, in order (lines with no header are rally lines)
Commenta line starting with # is ignored

Instruction numbers (not given to notes or checkpoints) run on continuously through the odometer check, rally sections and transit zones.

Speed, pause, suffix and general
FieldWhat it does
Speed (mph)Applies from that mileage on. Printed in the instruction as CAST 30.
PauseSeconds (30), mm:ss, or minutes (2m); printed as Pause 30. Or NEXTMIN for a next-minute out (Richta checkpoint type: Flying Start Next Minute): the car leaves at the next whole minute after it arrives (printed as Next minute out; the rally master sheet shows the out time).
SuffixExtra text printed at the end of the instruction, after the pause and CAST — e.g. FLR. Shown to participants.
GeneralRally master only — never printed for participants. Put CP in it to make the line a checkpoint: it is highlighted and labeled CP on the master sheet (with its CP time: the time since the previous checkpoint, or since the start / out time for the first checkpoint after a start or transit zone), gets no instruction number, and is left off the participant sheet entirely (so don't put speeds, pauses or suffixes on it).
Out times (OUTPOINT)
Out timeMeaning
TOD 09:50Time of day plus car number: car 1 goes out at 09:51, car 2 at 09:52. Participants see Out time 9:50 + car number.
NEXTMINFlying Start Next Minute (the Richta checkpoint type): the car goes out at the next whole minute after it arrives. Participants see Next minute out. Add the arrival time to assume for the master sheet (NEXTMIN 10:12:50).
Older formsA plain clock time (09:51:00) or +20 (minutes after the section starts) also still work.

Without a clock, NEXTMIN uses the time the car left the previous timed section (or the Start time under Rally master settings). The Car number under Rally master settings sets which car the times are shown for.

Text markup and mileage markers
MarkerWhat it does
**word**   *word*   ***word***Prints the words in bold, italic, or bold italic. Pause and CAST are bolded automatically.
\n in an instructionStarts a new line. Use \n\n for a blank line between paragraphs.
@ before a mileage@4.20 shows that mileage on the rally master output only.
Mileage precisionEnter mileages to the nearest 1/1000 mile (3.456). The sheets show and calculate with the value rounded to 1/100 mile (3.46, halves round up). What you typed is never changed: saving as text or CSV and the spreadsheet download keep the full entered value, and the spreadsheet formulas round it the same way.
Blank mileageLeave the mileage empty ( | Left at Y) while the route is still being measured. The Measure output shows every line and does no calculations. Participant and rally master output need the mileages.
Output types and printing
OutputWhat it shows
ParticipantInstruction numbers, mileage and instructions (with Pause, CAST and suffix). Checkpoints, General notes and master-only mileages are left out. Leg distances are optional.
Rally masterEvery line, including checkpoints and notes, plus time to instruction, elapsed, perfect time, out time and CP time, and an out point box for each timed leg.
MeasureEvery line in the participant layout, with blank mileage cells to write in. No calculations.

Page numbers print in the footer in Chrome and Edge (v131+); in other browsers turn on “Headers and footers” in the print dialog.

Files, header and spreadsheet backup

File header (optional): Title: ...   Subtitle: ...   Date: 2026-11-21   Start time: 09:30:00, one per line, usually at the top. When a file is loaded or pasted they fill in the form fields below and are removed from the text. Save as text file writes them back in.

Loading: a .txt file in this format, or a .csv with the columns mileage, instruction, speed, pause, suffix, general (header row optional; cells may contain line breaks; rows like Title,My Rally or Date,2026-11-21 set the title area).

Saving: Save as text file or Save as CSV keeps your route (reload it later with Load file).

Spreadsheet backup: Download spreadsheet (CSV with formulas) saves every line plus formulas for the instruction text, perfect times, out times and checkpoint times. Open it in Excel or Google Sheets and change a mileage, speed or pause (or the start time or car number at the top) and everything recalculates. Times that are typed in the sheet (start time, out clocks) should stay in hh:mm:ss form.

The car the times are worked out for. It only matters for out points written as TOD (out time = time of day + car number minutes): with TOD 09:50, car 1 goes out at 09:51 and car 2 at 09:52.
